# Contract details

## What semantic validation rejects

Schema validity is the easy half. These are the checks that fail contracts written in a hurry. Satisfy them while writing rather than discovering them at the gate:

- every dependency ID resolves to a milestone or finding that exists;
- an `accepted` ADR identifies exactly one selected option;
- a `blocked` progress report names the blocker and who clears it;
- research claiming high confidence states its strongest counterpoint;
- every diagram edge's `from` and `to` match a declared node `id`;
- every action and open question has an owner.

Treat warnings as work. A warning means the artifact is renderable and still not honest.

## Correcting defects

Correct semantic content in the JSON. Correct visual-system defects in the engine. Never hand-edit generated HTML. The next render discards it.
